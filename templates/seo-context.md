## Business overview

What the business does, who it is for, the core claim, target markets and languages, the site's stage (new, established, migrating, recovering from a drop), CMS or framework and how pages are published.

## Current goal

One measurable SEO goal with a timeframe. Examples: "increase non-branded organic signups 30 percent by Q2", "rank top 10 for 20 buying-intent terms by June", "recover the traffic lost in the March migration".

## Positioning

## Writing preferences
