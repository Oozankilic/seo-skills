## Rules

- Three to five internal links per article; one or two to a pillar, two or three to related articles, at most one product page, resources when mentioned.
- Descriptive anchors; vary them; never "click here".
- Cluster articles link to their pillar; pillars link to two or three cluster articles.
- No more than two links per paragraph; spread links through the body.
